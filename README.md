## About Me

I work on robot skill learning, behavior trees, foundation models for robotics,
and causality-aware autonomous decision making. My goal is to build robot skill
and behavior coordination systems that can be deployed and scaled in the real
world — and one day, robots with human-level tool-use and craftsmanship.

## Research Interests

- Robot skill learning for manipulation, tool-use, and craftsmanship
- Behavior trees for reactive manipulation and task planning
- Causality-aware autonomous decision making
